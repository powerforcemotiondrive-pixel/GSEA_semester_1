# 1. SEMESTER FRAMEWORK & ASSESSMENT ARCHITECTURE
## *Cadre Général & Modalités d'Évaluation*

### 1.1 General Semester Purpose (English)
Semester 1 focuses on developing practical, professional, and operational linguistic competencies to prepare students for their future responsibilities in civil aviation engineering, aerodrome management, and air transport operations. Rather than passive theoretical instruction, this course is communicative, interactive, and task-based. Students build active spoken fluency, listening precision, professional presentation skills, and grammatical accuracy through weekly spoken discussions, collaborative simulations, and self-evaluation logs in their digital Learning Portfolio. Progress is assessed through continuous assessment and three modular Formative Evaluations (FE1, FE2, FE3). At the end of the semester, students' overall performance, portfolio completion, and oral competence culminate in a formal **Go / No-Go** validation decision for progression.

### 1.2 Finalité Générale du Semestre & Décision Go / No-Go (Français)
Le semestre 1 a pour objectif de développer et de consolider les compétences linguistiques, professionnelles et opérationnelles indispensables aux futurs cadres, ingénieurs et techniciens de l'aviation civile (GSEA / ENAC). Privilégiant une approche communicative et actionnelle, les cours mettent l'accent sur la fluidité orale, l'aisance interactive, la précision technique et la justesse grammaticale à travers des études de cas hebdomadaires, des simulations professionnelles et la tenue d'un portfolio numérique d'apprentissage. L'évaluation repose sur un contrôle continu régulier et trois évaluations formatives orales (EF1, EF2, EF3). À l'issue du semestre, l'ensemble du travail de l'étudiant, son implication en classe et la validation de son portfolio déterminent la décision officielle **GO / NO GO** validant le passage au semestre suivant.

### 1.3 Assessment Architecture & Grade Formula
- **Continuous Assessment (50% of semester grade)**:
  - Active spoken participation and interaction in classroom sessions.
  - Completion and depth of the Digital Learning Portfolio (weekly self-reflection, vocabulary acquisition, and TOEIC tracking).
  - Quick-fire spoken briefs, vocabulary quizzes, and collaborative group tasks.
- **Formative Evaluations / FE Profiles (50% of semester grade)**:
  - **FE1 (Session 05)**: 30-minute paired oral presentation & technical Q&A (/20).
  - **FE2 (Session 10)**: 30-minute paired oral evaluation & case study defense (/20).
  - **FE3 (Session 15)**: 30-minute paired oral evaluation & operational press briefing (/20).
- **Session 16**: Global Semester Revision, Portfolio Clinic & Oral Exam Workshop.
- **Session 17**: Final Summative Evaluation / Oral Profile Validation (**Go / No-Go** decision).

# 2. OFFICIAL DETAILED SEMESTER OBJECTIVES
## *Objectifs Détaillés du Semestre*

### 2.1 General English (AG • LV101E) — 7 Core Competencies

#### 🇬🇧 English
1. **Continuous Assessment & Autonomous Learning**: Actively participate in weekly communicative tasks, role-plays, and self-evaluation logs in the Formative Learning Portfolio to track linguistic progression.
2. **Professional Development & Public Speaking**: Deliver structured, convincing oral presentations on contemporary society and mobility themes with professional signposting and visual commentary.
3. **Vocabulary Range & Lexical Precision**: Accurately recognize, pronounce, and employ an extensive range of B2 general and academic vocabulary related to transport evolution, climate transition, and tech ethics.
4. **Spoken Fluency & Spontaneous Argumentation**: Express viewpoints, construct balanced arguments, and defend positions smoothly during group discussions without noticeable strain or hesitation.
5. **Communicative Confidence & Autonomy**: Initiate conversations, ask clarifying questions, justify decisions, and resolve communication breakdowns independently in unfamiliar professional contexts.
6. **TOEIC Test Strategy & Performance Progression**: Master test-taking strategies for TOEIC Listening and Reading, analyzing dialogues, graphs, and business memos under timed conditions toward graduation requirements.
7. **Grammatical Accuracy & Structural Control**: Apply key structures (causatives, mixed conditionals, modal verbs of deduction, relative clauses, and cohesive linkers) with minimal interference.

#### 🇫🇷 Français
1. **Contrôle continu et apprentissage en autonomie** : Participer activement aux exercices communicatifs, jeux de rôle et renseigner régulièrement son portfolio numérique pour mesurer sa progression.
2. **Développement professionnel et prise de parole en public** : Structurer et présenter un exposé oral convaincant sur des thématiques contemporaines avec des connecteurs formels et des supports visuels adaptés.
3. **Étendue lexicale et précision terminologique** : Maîtriser et réutiliser un large répertoire de vocabulaire B2 général et académique lié aux mobilités, à la transition écologique et à l'éthique technologique.
4. **Aisance orale et argumentation spontanée** : Exprimer des opinions nuancées, construire des arguments contradictoires et débattre avec fluidité sans recherche laborieuse de formulations.
5. **Confiance communicative et autonomie** : Prendre la parole avec assurance, poser des questions de clarification, justifier ses choix et surmonter les blocages linguistiques avec aisance.
6. **Stratégie et progression au test TOEIC** : Développer des réflexes d'écoute et de lecture rapide (écrémage, repérage) pour atteindre et dépasser le niveau cible exigé pour l'obtention du diplôme.
7. **Justesse grammaticale et maîtrise syntaxique** : Employer les structures clés (verbes causatifs, conditionnels mixtes, modaux de déduction, propositions relatives) avec rigueur et clarté.

### 2.2 Technical English (AT • LV121E) — 6 ICAO Core Competencies

#### 🇬🇧 English
1. **Aeronautical Technical Terminology**: Accurately define and deploy standard aviation terminology regarding aerodromes, runway safety, ground handling, airframe structures, flight physics, and weather.
2. **Continuous Assessment & Formative Evaluations**: Validate regular linguistic and technical progression through weekly case studies and three 30-minute oral evaluation tutorials (Airports, Aircraft/Airlines, Press Briefings).
3. **Aviation Culture & Operational Environment**: Understand and articulate the global commercial aviation ecosystem, passenger touchpoints, turnaround milestones, safety management systems, and environmental challenges.
4. **Technical Fluency & Operational Briefings**: Deliver concise spoken summaries, pre-flight defect reports, and shift handovers using formal cause-and-effect structures and clear logical sequencers without reading notes.
5. **Operational Confidence & Problem-Solving**: Communicate confidently during non-routine operations, passenger disruptions (EU261), flight diversions, and ramp incidents, resolving ambiguities calmly and precisely.
6. **ICAO Language Proficiency Dimensions (Level 4/5)**: Consolidate communicative competencies across the six ICAO rating dimensions: Pronunciation (intelligible), Structure (controlled), Vocabulary (varied), Fluency (natural), Comprehension (accurate), and Interactions (immediate).

#### 🇫🇷 Français
1. **Terminologie technique aéronautique** : Comprendre, définir et réutiliser avec rigueur le vocabulaire aéronautique standard relatif aux aérodromes, aux pistes, à l'assistance en escale, aux systèmes cellule et à la météo.
2. **Contrôle continu et évaluations formatives** : Valider une progression linguistique régulière grâce aux études de cas hebdomadaires et à trois évaluations formatives orales de 30 minutes (Aéroports, Aéronefs, Actualité).
3. **Culture aéronautique générale et environnement opérationnel** : Expliquer le fonctionnement global du secteur aérien : rôles des acteurs (aéroports, compagnies, assistance, navigation aérienne), parcours passager et sécurité des vols.
4. **Aisance orale et synthèses techniques** : Présenter des signalements d'anomalies lors du tour avion et des retours d'expérience avec concision et fluidité, en maîtrisant les relations de cause à effet.
5. **Confiance opérationnelle et résolution d'imprévus** : Communiquer avec assurance lors de situations perturbées (règlementation EU261, déroutements, retards en escale) et vérifier une information avec clarté.
6. **Critères de compétence linguistique OACI (Niveau 4/5)** : Consolider les 6 critères OACI : Prononciation (intelligible), Structure (maîtrisée), Vocabulaire (étendu), Aisance (naturelle), Compréhension (précise) et Interactions (réactives).

### MODULE 1: TRAVEL, TRANSPORT & SOCIETY

#### SESSION 01 — Introduction & Personal Travel Anecdotes
- **English Overview**: Icebreaker and diagnostic session. Students introduce themselves, share authentic travel anecdotes, and analyze passenger disruption scenarios. Focus on storytelling narrative tenses and polite customer-service de-escalation.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Séance d'introduction et de diagnostic. Présentation des étudiants, récits d'expériences de voyage et analyse d'incidents passagers. Travail sur les temps du récit et la gestion des réclamations.
- **Interactive Activities**: Paired speed-networking interview, "Two Truths and a Travel Mishap" storytelling circle, passenger rights de-escalation role-play.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Travel idioms (*get off to a flying start, off the beaten track, hit the road, word of mouth*), travel collocations (*book in advance, clear customs, miss a connection*).
  - *Grammar*: Narrative tenses contrast (Past Simple vs. Past Continuous vs. Past Perfect). Uncountable nouns check (*luggage*, *travel*, *accommodation*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Past tense regular verb endings (`-ed` pronounced /t/, /d/, or /ɪd/), word stress in travel compound nouns.

#### SESSION 02 — Evolution of Transport & Sustainable Mobility
- **English Overview**: Examination of multimodal transport systems (rail vs. air vs. road) across a 600 km corridor. Introduction to sustainable mobility (DDRS) and transport infrastructure planning.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Étude des systèmes de transport multimodaux (ferroviaire, aérien, routier) sur un corridor de 600 km. Introduction aux mobilités durables (DDRS) et à l'aménagement des infrastructures.
- **Interactive Activities**: Multi-modal route optimization debate, business travel carbon-budgeting challenge, timed TOEIC reading practice (Part 7 transport schedules).
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Modal shift, carbon footprint, high-speed rail corridor, last-mile connectivity, intermodality, transit-oriented development.
  - *Grammar*: Expressing cause and effect (*owing to, as a result of, consequently, lead to*), double comparatives (*the faster the train, the lower the emissions*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Stress shifts in word families (*'transport* [noun] vs. *trans'port* [verb]; *'mobility* / *'infrastructure*).

#### SESSION 03 — Overtourism & Destination Management
- **English Overview**: Analysis of the economic benefits versus social and ecological damage of mass tourism in historic cities and sensitive natural sites (Venice, Amsterdam, Maya Bay).
- **Aperçu en Français** : Analyse des impacts économiques face aux dégradations sociales et écologiques du surtourisme dans les villes historiques et espaces protégés.
- **Interactive Activities**: Stakeholder role-play debate (local residents, municipal authorities, cruise ship operators, hoteliers), drafting city regulation bylaws.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Carrying capacity, visitor quotas, tourist levy/tax, local resident displacement, season peak, UNESCO preservation.
  - *Grammar*: Causative verbs of authority (*have/make/get something done* — *"The city had the cruise terminal moved"*), concessive linkers (*despite, in spite of, whereas*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Syllable stress and clear vowel reductions in long abstract nouns (*de-stur-i-za-'tion*, *re-gu-'la-tion*, *sus-tain-a-'bi-li-ty*).

#### SESSION 04 — Ecotourism, Greenwashing & Destination Pitch (Prep FE1)
- **English Overview**: Distinguishing authentic community-led ecotourism from commercial greenwashing. Students pitch a sustainable travel initiative in small groups as direct preparation for FE1.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Distinguer l'écotourisme authentique du greenwashing commercial. Présentation en équipe d'un projet de voyage éco-responsable en vue de l'EF1.
- **Interactive Activities**: "Green or Greenwashed?" corporate audit, collaborative micro-project design sprint, mock peer evaluations with timing constraints.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Carbon offsetting, wildlife sanctuary, low-impact hospitality, local empowerment, biodiversity monitoring, greenwashing claims.
  - *Grammar*: Mixed conditionals (*"If the national park had invested in solar shuttles earlier, it would attract more eco-conscious tourists today"*), clauses of purpose (*so that, in order to, provided that*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Intonation in persuasive pitches, emphatic stress on quantitative benefits (*"over sixty percent reduction"*).

#### SESSION 05 — FORMATIVE EVALUATION 1 (FE1): Oral Tutorials
- **English Overview**: Graded 30-minute oral evaluation in pairs with the course teacher. Students present a sustainable travel destination profile with visual slides, answer spontaneous questions, and review their portfolio vocabulary log.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Épreuve orale notée de 30 minutes en binôme. Présentation d'un projet d'écotourisme avec diaporama, échange spontané de questions-réponses et revue du portfolio de vocabulaire.
- **Assessment Criteria**: Fluency & interaction (5 pts), lexical range & precision (5 pts), grammatical accuracy (5 pts), presentation structure & pronunciation (5 pts).

### MODULE 2: WEATHER, ENVIRONMENT & ECOLOGICAL TRANSITION

#### SESSION 06 — Extreme Weather & Atmospheric Hazards
- **English Overview**: Exploration of extreme meteorological phenomena (heatwaves, flash floods, hurricanes, severe turbulence) and their disruption to global transport infrastructure and community safety.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Étude des phénomènes météorologiques extrêmes (canicules, inondations éclairs, tempêtes) et de leurs impacts sur les réseaux de transport et les populations.
- **Interactive Activities**: Emergency weather response simulation, interpreting climate trend infographics, TOEIC Listening practice (Part 4 emergency weather broadcasts).
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Torrential downpour, storm surge, heat dome, infrastructure vulnerability, early warning system, contingency plan.
  - *Grammar*: Passive voice in technical crisis reporting (*"Power lines were severed; transport links have been suspended"*), modals of deduction (*must have experienced, could be caused by*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Difficult consonant clusters in meteorological terms (*draughts, bursts, depths, precipitation*).

#### SESSION 07 — Sustainable Cities & Low-Emission Urban Mobility
- **English Overview**: Architectural and urban planning innovations for climate resilience: the 15-minute city concept, sponge cities, low-emission zones (LEZ), and green transit grids.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Innovations urbaines et architecturales pour la résilience climatique : le concept de ville du quart d'heure, les villes éponges, les zones à faibles émissions (ZFE) et les transports propres.
- **Interactive Activities**: Redesigning an urban transport corridor, comparing European transit models, TOEIC Reading practice (Part 7 urban development proposals).
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Permeable pavement, urban heat island, congestion charging, active mobility (cycling/walking), mixed-use zoning, district heating.
  - *Grammar*: Quantifiers and determiners (*a great deal of, negligible amounts of, fewer vehicles vs. less pollution*), complex noun phrases.
  - *Pronunciation*: Contrastive sentence stress when comparing figures and city statistics.

#### SESSION 08 — Building & Supporting Environmental Arguments
- **English Overview**: Professional argumentation workshop. Developing structured, evidence-based environmental arguments, handling counter-arguments diplomatically, and avoiding logical fallacies.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Atelier d'argumentation professionnelle. Structurer une prise de position environnementale fondée sur des données fiables, gérer les contre-arguments avec diplomatie et débattre en groupe.
- **Interactive Activities**: The Great Carbon Tax Debate, rapid-fire argument rebuttal pairs, analyzing argumentative rhetorical strategies in TED-style talks.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Counter-argument, regulatory mandate, public pushback, unintended consequence, precedent, viable alternative.
  - *Grammar*: Verbs followed by gerunds vs. infinitives (*advocate implementing, fail to address, consider adopting*), diplomatic signposting (*"While I concede that..., the data indicates..."*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Polite vs. aggressive intonation patterns during intellectual disagreement.

#### SESSION 09 — Biodiversity in Urban Areas & Transport Hubs (Prep FE2)
- **English Overview**: Ecological engineering around airports and transport hubs: wildlife corridors, green roofs, runway bird strike prevention, and preserving living ecosystems.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Ingénierie écologique autour des aéroports et pôles d'échange : corridors écologiques, toitures végétalisées, prévention du péril aviaire et préservation de la biodiversité.
- **Interactive Activities**: Airport eco-corridor feasibility study, group rehearsal of the FE2 Climate Resilience Redesign, peer feedback rubric check.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Native species, ecological corridor, bird strike hazard, wetland restoration, habitat fragmentation, biodiversity net gain.
  - *Grammar*: Complex relative clauses (*non-defining vs. defining: "The retention ponds, which were excavated in 2022, provide..."*), prepositions following nouns and adjectives.
  - *Pronunciation*: Word stress in biological and environmental nomenclature.

#### SESSION 10 — FORMATIVE EVALUATION 2 (FE2): Climate Resilience Redesign
- **English Overview**: Graded 30-minute oral tutorial in pairs. Students pitch an urban or transport hub Climate Resilience Redesign project, defend technical choices against budget/climate constraints, and present their updated portfolio reflections.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Épreuve orale notée de 30 minutes en binôme. Présentation d'un projet de résilience climatique pour un aéroport ou une ville, argumentation face aux contraintes et revue du portfolio.
- **Assessment Criteria**: Environmental analysis (5 pts), spoken fluency & argumentation (5 pts), grammatical complexity & accuracy (5 pts), interactive handling of teacher questions (5 pts).

### MODULE 1: AERODROMES, AIR TRAFFIC & SURFACE OPERATIONS

#### SESSION 01 — Introduction, Aviation Survey & Regulatory Modals
- **English Overview**: Introduction to the commercial aviation ecosystem. Students examine current aviation headlines, debate airline operating models (low-cost vs. legacy carriers), and master regulatory modal verbs of obligation, prohibition, and authorization under ICAO Doc 9835 criteria.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Introduction à l'écosystème de l'aviation commerciale. Analyse de l'actualité aéronautique, comparaison des modèles économiques des compagnies et maîtrise des modaux d'obligation et d'autorisation selon les critères OACI.
- **Interactive Activities**: Aviation current events warm-up, unruly passenger policy debate, customer-service disruption role-play under EU261 regulations, 60-second spoken policy briefing.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Go-around, touchdown zone, apron, landing gear, turnaround, unruly passenger, low-cost carrier (LCC), ancillary revenue.
  - *Grammar*: Regulatory modals (*must, must not, is required to, is authorized to, may not, shall*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Syllable stress in aviation terms (*'apron, 'runway, 'turboprop, 'diverted*).

#### SESSION 02 — Passenger Journey & Airfield Surface Movement
- **English Overview**: Tracing traveler facilitation from landside curb to airside boarding gate. Decoding aerodrome physical movement areas: runway designators, taxiway markings, holding points, and surface signage. Managing airside security incursions.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Analyse du parcours passager du côté ville (landside) au côté piste (airside). Décodage du balisage et de la signalisation d'aérodrome (pistes, taxiways, points d'arrêt). Gestion des intrusions côté piste.
- **Interactive Activities**: Passenger concourse flow diagramming, airfield surface map navigation, unauthorized airside access de-escalation role-play, 60-second magnetic heading briefing.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Landside vs. airside, concourse, security checkpoint, holding point, taxiway centerline, threshold markings, runway designator (e.g., 14L/32R), FOD (Foreign Object Debris).
  - *Grammar*: Spatial prepositions of point, surface, and movement (*at the holding point, on the apron, taxiing via Charlie, lining up on Runway 26*).
  - *Pronunciation*: NATO phonetic alphabet pronunciation and precision in numerical runway headings (*tree, fife, niner*).

#### SESSION 03 — Ground Handling, Apron Operations & Turnaround
- **English Overview**: Deep-dive into airside apron operations and the critical turnaround window. Examining Ground Support Equipment (GSE), ramp personnel duties, and sequential milestones from wheel chocks to pushback.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Étude détaillée des opérations en escale sur l'aire de trafic. Découverte des engins d'assistance (GSE), des missions des personnels de piste et de la chronologie du demi-tour avion (touchdown au pushback).
- **Interactive Activities**: Identifying airside vehicles from functional diagrams, sequencing turnaround milestones on a Gantt chart, ramp delay negotiation role-play, delivering a 60-second shift handover briefing.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: GPU (Ground Power Unit), APU (Auxiliary Power Unit), pushback tug, baggage conveyor belt, refueler, Marshaller, TRC (Turnaround Coordinator), chocks.
  - *Grammar*: Time linkers and sequential markers (*simultaneously, prior to, upon completion of, concurrently*), expressing operational cause and consequence (*"Owing to a catering truck breakdown, boarding was delayed by 15 minutes"*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Clear distinction between acronyms (*GPU vs. APU, AODB, GSE*).

#### SESSION 04 — Jobs in Aviation & Institutional Jurisdiction (Prep FE1)
- **English Overview**: Comprehensive taxonomy of aviation professions across landside, airside, flight ops, and air navigation services. Clarifying the administrative and technical jurisdiction of TSEEAC personnel (DGAC) vs. tactical ATC controllers.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Panorama complet des métiers de l'aérien. Distinction institutionnelle et opérationnelle des fonctions TSEEAC (DGAC) par rapport aux contrôleurs aériens (ICNA).

- **Linguistic Focus**:

  - *Pronunciation*: Syllable stress in multi-syllabic professional titles (*ad-min-is-'tra-tion, in-'spec-tor, co-'or-di-na-tor*).

#### SESSION 05 — FORMATIVE EVALUATION 1 (FE1): Technical Airport Profile

### MODULE 2: AIRCRAFT & AERODYNAMICS

#### SESSION 06 — Aerodynamics & Fundamental Flight Principles
- **English Overview**: The physics of atmospheric flight. Explaining the four fundamental forces (Lift, Weight, Thrust, Drag) in equilibrium, airfoil anatomy, Bernoulli's principle, and angle of attack dynamics.
- **Aperçu en Français** : La physique du vol atmosphérique. Explication des quatre forces fondamentales (portance, poids, poussée, traînée) en équilibre, profil d'aile, principe de Bernoulli et angle d'incidence.
- **Interactive Activities**: Airflow vector diagramming, pilot-to-engineer aerodynamic flight control briefing, analyzing stall dynamics and aerodynamic recovery procedures.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Lift, weight/gravity, thrust, drag, airfoil, camber, chord line, angle of attack (AoA), boundary layer, stall, vortex.
  - *Grammar*: Zero and first conditionals in flight physics (*"If the angle of attack exceeds 16 degrees, the boundary layer separates and the wing stalls"*), cause-and-effect connectors.
  - *Pronunciation*: Accurate pronunciation of technical nouns (*'chord, ae-ro-'dy-na-mics, 'drag, e-qui-'li-bri-um*).

#### SESSION 07 — Aircraft Anatomy: Structure & Systems
- **English Overview**: Airframe anatomy, structural primary materials, and onboard technical systems. Identifying the three aircraft axes (pitch, roll, yaw) and their corresponding primary and secondary flight control surfaces.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Anatomie de l'aéronef, matériaux de structure et servitudes de bord. Identification des trois axes de vol (tangage, roulis, lacet) et des gouvernes principales et secondaires associées.
- **Interactive Activities**: Cockpit and fuselage exterior diagram labelling, walkaround inspection simulation reporting airframe damage (dents, bird strikes, hydraulic leaks), Minimum Equipment List (MEL) dispatch check.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Fuselage, empennage, aileron, elevator, rudder, flaps, slats, spoilers, radome, pitot tube, winglet, hydraulic actuator.
  - *Grammar*: Spatial and functional descriptors (*"located along the trailing edge of the wing", "designed to increase camber during low-speed approach"*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Stress in aircraft components (*'aileron, 'rudder, 'fuselage, em-pen-'nage*).

#### SESSION 08 — Commercial Aircraft Families & Airline Fleet Economics
- **English Overview**: Categorization of commercial transport aircraft: regional turboprops, single-aisle narrowbodies, twin-aisle widebodies, and freighters. Comparing manufacturer strategies (Airbus vs. Boeing) and airline route economics (hub-and-spoke vs. point-to-point).
- **Aperçu en Français** : Catégorisation des avions commerciaux : turbopropulseurs régionaux, moyen-courriers monocouloirs, gros-porteurs long-courriers et avions cargos. Comparaison des constructeurs (Airbus vs. Boeing) et des modèles de réseaux de lignes.
- **Interactive Activities**: Aircraft specification comparison challenge, simulated Airline Fleet Selection Committee pitch, evaluating fuel-burn and seat-mile costs.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: Narrowbody, widebody, MTOW (Maximum Takeoff Weight), payload-range, seat-mile cost, fuel burn, fleet commonality, retrofitting.
  - *Grammar*: Comparative and superlative structures with modifiers (*substantially lower operating cost, marginally longer range, significantly quieter engines*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Numerical aircraft designations (*A-three-fifty, Boeing seven-eight-seven*).

#### SESSION 09 — Landmark Aviation Accidents & Technical Causal Analysis (Prep FE2)
- **English Overview**: Human factors, CRM, and technical accident investigation. Analysis of landmark historical aviation occurrences (Tenerife 1977, BA 38, US Airways 1549, AF 447). Extracting lessons learned and safety enhancements.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Facteurs humains, gestion des ressources de l'équipage (CRM) et enquêtes de sécurité. Analyse d'accidents historiques majeurs et des recommandations de sécurité qui en ont découlé.
- **Interactive Activities**: 15-minute collaborative group research sprint on an assigned accident, delivering a 5-minute technical safety debrief to peers, rehearsal for FE2.
- **Linguistic Focus**:
  - *Vocabulary*: FDR (Flight Data Recorder), CVR (Cockpit Voice Recorder), CFIT (Controlled Flight Into Terrain), pitot icing, spatial disorientation, causal chain, safety recommendation.
  - *Grammar*: Narrative past tenses in chronological investigations (*Past Simple, Past Continuous, Past Perfect*), modal verbs of past deduction (*"The crew must have misinterpreted the stall warning"*).
  - *Pronunciation*: Syllable stress in investigative terminology (*in-ves-ti-'ga-tion, ca-tas-'tro-phic, 'cri-ti-cal*).

#### SESSION 10 — FORMATIVE EVALUATION 2 (FE2): Aircraft & Airlines Profile

- **Aperçu en Français** : Urgences médicales à bord, facteurs humains, physiologie du vol, gestion de la fatigue et désorientation spatiale. Application de la grille IMSAFE et protocoles de déroutement médical.

#### SESSION 12 — Meteorology & Severe Weather in Commercial Aviation
- **English Overview**: Evaluating the operational impact of severe weather on flight planning and aerodrome operations. Decoding operational METAR, TAF, and SIGMET reports. Understanding convective microbursts, wind shear, and structural icing.
- **Aperçu en Français** : Impacts opérationnels de la météo sévère sur la gestion des vols et des pistes. Décodage des messages METAR, TAF et SIGMET. Étude des cisaillements de vent, micro-rafales et givrage structurel.

## 5. INSTRUCTIONAL SUMMARY & GOVERNANCE RULES

1. **Language Practice, Not Theoretical Lectures**: Every session is centered around spoken student interaction, peer collaboration, and realistic aviation or societal scenarios. Teachers act as facilitators, monitors, and language coaches.
2. **Zero Academic or Pedagogical Jargon**: All classroom worksheets, slides, and teacher prompts use clear, plain B2 English (*"Useful phrases"*, *"Connecting words"*, *"Example story"*) without academic meta-language.
3. **The Learning Portfolio is Integral**: Student reflections and vocabulary logs recorded in the digital portfolio contribute directly to continuous assessment and the final Go / No-Go validation.
4. **SCORM & Offline Parity**: All materials referenced in this guide are mirrored within the offline digital deck and tracked in the system manifest for 100% offline self-containment.
