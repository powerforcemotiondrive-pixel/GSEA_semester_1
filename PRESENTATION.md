## Slide 6: Pedagogical Philosophy & Environmental Impact

- **Badge**: SLIDE 6 • PEDAGOGY &amp; SUSTAINABILITY
- **Subtitle**: L'Approche par Comp&eacute;tences: Building Operational Confidence, Eliminating Exam Stress & Saving Paper
- **Pill**: Zero-Print • Competency-Based

### Card 1: L'Approche par Compétences
- **Tag**: PEDAGOGICAL ARCHITECTURE
- **Metric**: Competency-Based

**Key Points**:
- **Grounded in TSEEAC Feedback:** Designed directly around operational feedback from civil aviation technicians who need spontaneous speaking confidence rather than theoretical grammar memorization.
- **Continuous Assessment (No Exams):** Traditional high-stakes end-of-semester written exams are replaced with continuous formative evaluation (FE) in small groups or pairs.
- **Building Confidence:** Low-anxiety environment encouraging active peer interaction, immediate constructive teacher feedback, and personal learning reflection.
- **Proactive Certification Success:** Integrated preparation for the **TOEIC** (mandatory CEFR B2 benchmark) and the **MTF** (mandatory civil aviation graduation exam).

### Card 2: Environmental Benefits of our Structure
- **Tag**: ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT
- **Metric**: Zero-Print Practice

**Key Points**:
- **Strict No-Print Approach:** Digital worksheets, auto-saving textareas, and on-screen rubrics completely eliminate physical photocopies (~2,000 pages of paper saved per promotion).
- **Printable PDFs Only on Demand:** Clean 7-page A4 PDF worksheets are generated on demand via native PDF engines for students who genuinely require physical copies.
- **Ultra-Low Energy Computing:** Client-side rendering requires no active server infrastructure or compute cycles, minimizing server-farm energy consumption.
- **Full DD&RS Integration:** Every module is linked to official French Higher Education ecological transition benchmarks.
