Course Syllabus & Curriculum Guide Référentiel de Compétences & Programme Détaillé

Official 17-Session Educational Roadmap • Continuous Assessment, Learning Portfolios & Go / No-Go Validation Programme officiel en 17 séances • Contrôle continu, Portfolio d'apprentissage & Validation Go / No-Go

SEMESTER 1 INSTRUCTIONAL FRAMEWORK CADRE PÉDAGOGIQUE DU SEMESTRE 1 Continuous Assessment (50%) + Formative Evaluations (50%) • Go / No-Go Decision Contrôle Continu (50%) + Évaluations Formatives (50%) • Décision Go / No-Go

Semester 1 focuses on developing practical, professional, and operational linguistic competencies to prepare students for their future responsibilities in civil aviation engineering, aerodrome management, and air transport operations. Rather than passive theoretical instruction, this course is communicative, interactive, and task-based. Students build active spoken fluency, listening precision, professional presentation skills, and grammatical accuracy through weekly spoken discussions, collaborative simulations, and self-evaluation logs in their digital Learning Portfolio. Progress is assessed through continuous assessment and three modular Formative Evaluations (FE1, FE2, FE3). At the end of the semester, students' overall performance, portfolio completion, and oral competence culminate in a formal Go / No-Go validation decision for progression. Le semestre 1 a pour objectif de développer et de consolider les compétences linguistiques, professionnelles et opérationnelles indispensables aux futurs cadres, ingénieurs et techniciens de l'aviation civile (GSEA / ENAC). Privilégiant une approche communicative et actionnelle, les cours mettent l'accent sur la fluidité orale, l'aisance interactive, la précision technique et la justesse grammaticale à travers des études de cas hebdomadaires, des simulations professionnelles et la tenue d'un portfolio numérique d'apprentissage. L'évaluation repose sur un contrôle continu régulier et trois évaluations formatives orales (EF1, EF2, EF3). À l'issue du semestre, l'ensemble du travail de l'étudiant, son implication en classe et la validation de son portfolio déterminent la décision officielle GO / NO GO validant le passage au semestre suivant.

01

Introduction & Travel Anecdotes Introduction & Récits de Voyages

Teaching Session

Diagnostic session and icebreaker. Students introduce themselves, share authentic travel anecdotes, and analyze passenger disruption scenarios. Séance de diagnostic et d'introduction. Présentation des étudiants, récits d'anecdotes de voyage et analyse d'incidents passagers.

🎯 Activities: 🎯 Activités : Paired speed-networking interview, "Two Truths and a Travel Mishap" storytelling circle, passenger rights de-escalation role-play. Entretiens croisés en binôme, cercle de narration "Deux vérités et un imprévu", jeu de rôle de gestion de réclamation client.
📚 Vocabulary Travel idioms (get off to a flying start, off the beaten track, hit the road, word of mouth), collocations (book in advance, clear customs, miss a connection).
⚙️ Grammar & Patterns Narrative past tenses contrast (Past Simple, Past Continuous, Past Perfect). Uncountable nouns check (luggage, accommodation).
🎙️ Pronunciation & Standards Regular verb past tense endings (-ed /t/, /d/, /ɪd/), word stress in travel compounds.
02

Evolution of Transport & Sustainable Mobility Évolution des Transports & Mobilités Durables

Teaching Session

Examination of multimodal transport systems (rail vs. air vs. road) across a 600 km corridor. Introduction to sustainable mobility (DDRS). Étude des systèmes de transport multimodaux (ferroviaire, aérien, routier) sur 600 km. Introduction aux mobilités durables (DDRS).

🎯 Activities: 🎯 Activités : Multi-modal route optimization debate, business travel carbon-budgeting challenge, timed TOEIC reading practice (Part 7 transport schedules). Débat d'optimisation multimodale, atelier d'éco-budget carbone professionnel, entraînement chronométré TOEIC Reading (Partie 7).
📚 Vocabulary Modal shift, carbon footprint, high-speed rail corridor, intermodality, transit-oriented development.
⚙️ Grammar & Patterns Expressing cause and effect (owing to, as a result of, lead to), double comparatives (the faster the train, the lower the emissions).
🎙️ Pronunciation & Standards Stress shifts in word families ('transport [noun] vs. trans'port [verb]; 'mobility / 'infrastructure).

Overtourism & Destination Management Surtourisme & Gestion des Destinations

📚 Vocabulary Carrying capacity, visitor quotas, tourist levy/tax, resident displacement, season peak, UNESCO preservation.
⚙️ Grammar & Patterns Causative verbs of authority (have/make/get something done), concessive linkers (despite, in spite of, whereas).
🎙️ Pronunciation & Standards Syllable stress and vowel reductions in long abstract nouns (de-stur-i-za-'tion, re-gu-'la-tion).
