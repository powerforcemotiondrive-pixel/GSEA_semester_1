Evaluation Strategy SLIDE 06 / 12

Continuous Assessment & TOEIC (Final GO / NO GO)

Replacing high-stakes written exams with continuous formative tracking, peer interaction, and structured feedback:

1

Continuous In-Class Work

Evaluated on active participation in speaking tasks, role-play debates, proper use of technical vocabulary, and consistent homework completion.

2

GlobalExam TOEIC Milestones (~30 hrs)

Every student has an individual account from Week 1. Teachers monitor self-paced listening & reading progress at 3 set milestones during the term.

3

Small Project Presentations (FE1 • FE2 • FE3)

Dedicated 30-minute oral tutorials at the end of each module with alternating pairs and trios to evaluate technical vocabulary in context.

4

End-of-Semester Review Test

A short consolidated in-class vocabulary and concept review test verifying that all essential terms are mastered for the final GO / NO GO decision.

Development Methodology SLIDE 08 / 12

From Paper Sketch to AI-Powered Learning Portal: The Creation Workflow

How pedagogical necessity drove rapid technical innovation through human vision and agentic AI execution:

1

The Hand-Drawn Sketch

We started with a simple paper drawing of the ideal student cockpit interface, designed from the ground up to eliminate cluttered Moodle page scrolling, lost attachments, and static PDF links.

2

Moodle Display Exploration

We investigated various display options within eCampus and landed on SCORM 1.2 / Single-File Display: an interactive, self-contained mini-app running seamlessly inside Moodle with automatic grade sync.

3

Lightweight Native Web Standards

We rejected heavy, fragile web frameworks (React, Angular, bulky npm runtimes). We chose native HTML5, modern vanilla JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS for extreme lightness (< 7 MB) and 10+ year longevity.

4

Iterative AI-Agent Development (Gemini)

We fully exploited advanced AI agents (Gemini) to build the initial codebase, iterating rapidly through micro-sprints and rigorous stress-testing until arriving at a rock-solid, crash-proof architecture.

Self-Contained Mini-App • Fast Iterative Cycles Human Pedagogy + AI Power

Structural Safeguards: Technological Longevity & GDPR Privacy

Addressing core institutional risks with zero-compromise technical protections:

Timeless Web Standards & SCORM 1.2

  • • Built on pure, timeless standards: HTML5, native ES6 JavaScript, and standard CSS.
  • • Zero dependencies on third-party frameworks (no React, Angular, or Storyline blobs that deprecate or break).

Student Data Privacy & GDPR Compliance

  • • 100% anonymous Git codebase (students identified strictly as GRPA001–GRPB009).
